## The pitch

> We sell naming rights to potholes: brands pay to sponsor the crater outside your office, and the city finally fixes it — because a pothole named after a burger chain gets filled in a week.

One sentence, 188 characters. Bizarre at first glance — selling the naming rights to a hole in the road — and real by the second: brands pay for the sponsorship, the city gets the repair funded, and a pothole with a corporate name on it gets fixed in a week.
